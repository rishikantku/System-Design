Interview mode

How the simulator works

Mode B for every question on the study page
  1. Pick a question or a mock, a time mode (60 is the real module), and a hint level.
  2. Run ten rounds: problem → implement → requirement change → test → "would you ship this?" on flawed AI output → then the follow-up engine, level by level: correctness, complexity, concurrency, scale, production. Answer out loud, use your real assistant (CoderPad sandbox, or Claude in another window), and log what you said and the prompts you sent.
  3. Review: in round 5 you decide ship or not and name the defect before it is revealed — then grade your own review.
  4. Score on the Staff standard: 15 dimensions and 7 AI-collaboration checks → eight headline scores out of 10 → PASS / BORDERLINE / FAIL → your three biggest gaps, each linked to the exercise that trains it.
  5. Second opinion: "Grade with Claude" copies the whole transcript and the standard. Save feeds the weakness tracker.
What it can and cannot doThis page cannot read your answers and judge them — nothing static can, and pretending would be worse than useless. It runs the structure, keeps the time, hides the answers, and records your scores. For a live interviewer that reacts to what you say, use Copy interviewer prompt and run the round in a Claude chat; the prompt tells Claude not to reveal solutions, to show flawed AI output and ask "would you ship this?", to ask C# questions only about what you used, and to score on the same standard.

Scoring

The Staff Engineer standard

Verdict rulesPASS — the mean of the eight headline scores ≥ 7.0, and AI Collaboration ≥ 6, and Staff-Level Judgment ≥ 6, and AI-output verification ≥ 3, and nothing scored 1.
BORDERLINE — mean ≥ 5.5, or a passing mean that failed a gate.
FAIL — anything else.

The gates are the point: working code with blindly accepted AI output fails the verification gate and cannot pass, however high everything else is. This calibration is mine. The only published scale is Hello Interview's report of a 4-point scale where 3 passes.
