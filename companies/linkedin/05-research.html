How to read this

Something is a reported question only when a candidate described being asked it, or when you supplied it from your own collection. Preparation guides, "top LinkedIn questions" articles and recruiter advice are never counted as reports — they sit in the general topics section instead.

<span class="tag med">conf</span> MEDIUMOne strong recent report, or several older ones
<span class="tag low">conf</span> LOWSingle report, thin detail, or old
recurringTwo or more independent sources — copies of the same original report do not count twice
singleOne source. Useful practice, not a pattern
