How to use this course

Short, single-pattern videos you can revise from the night before.
Every lesson is its own video, twelve to eighteen minutes. That is deliberate: a pattern you are shaky on should cost you one lesson to revise, not a scrub bar through a five-hour file. Each lesson runs the same eighteen beats — the question as an interviewer poses it, the interview context, how to think about it, the naive answer, why the naive answer is not good enough, the key observation, the optimal approach, why it works, a visual walkthrough, a worked example, edge cases, the C# code, a line-by-line walk of that code, time, space, follow-ups, how the interviewer may modify the question, and how to respond. There are pause-and-think points in every lesson. Use them. The video waits; an interviewer does not, and the only way to build the reflex is to do the thinking before you hear the answer.

What to pair each lesson with

The tracker

Before a lesson — read the matching item on <a href="01-coding.html">01 Staff Coding</a> and try the problem cold for ten minutes. A lesson you have already struggled with teaches four times as much.

Mark it

After a lesson — mark the item on 01 Staff Coding: solved, needs revision, slow, or needed a hint. The dashboard readiness bar reads those marks, so the course and the tracker stay one system.

The research

For the evidence behind a relevance header — <a href="05-research.html">05 Recent interview research</a> has the source, the date and the role for every reported question, plus the ones that are general preparation rather than reported.
